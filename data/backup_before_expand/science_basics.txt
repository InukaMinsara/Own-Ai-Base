Science is the systematic study of the natural world.
Physics studies matter, energy, motion, and forces.
Chemistry studies substances and how they interact.
Biology studies living organisms.
Gravity is a force that attracts objects toward one another.
Energy is the capacity to cause change.
Matter has mass and occupies space.
Atoms are basic units of matter.
Molecules are groups of atoms bonded together.
Cells are the basic structural units of living organisms.
Plants use photosynthesis to convert light energy into chemical energy.
Water can exist as a solid, liquid, or gas.
The scientific method uses observation, questions, hypotheses, experiments, and analysis.
