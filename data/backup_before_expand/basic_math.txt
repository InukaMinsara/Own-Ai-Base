Addition combines numbers to find a total.
Subtraction finds the difference between numbers.
Multiplication represents repeated addition.
Division separates a quantity into equal parts.
A fraction represents a part of a whole.
A percentage is a number expressed as a fraction of one hundred.
An equation is a mathematical statement that two expressions are equal.
A variable can represent an unknown value.
A triangle has three sides.
A square has four equal sides.
The area of a rectangle is its length multiplied by its width.
The perimeter of a rectangle is twice the sum of its length and width.
A prime number has exactly two positive divisors.
Zero is neither positive nor negative.
