Computers are electronic machines that process information.
A computer uses hardware and software to perform tasks.
The CPU is responsible for executing instructions.
RAM is temporary memory used by running programs.
Storage keeps data even when the computer is turned off.
A GPU is designed to perform many calculations in parallel.
Artificial intelligence is a field of computer science.
Machine learning allows computers to learn patterns from data.
Neural networks are mathematical models inspired by networks of neurons.
A dataset is a collection of information used for analysis or training.
