Knowledge is organized information that can be learned, tested, and applied.
Earth is the third planet from the Sun and has one natural satellite, the Moon.
The atmosphere is a layer of gases surrounding a planet.
A continent is a large continuous landmass.
A country is a political and geographic territory governed by institutions.
A map is a representation of geographic information.
A calendar organizes time into units such as days, weeks, months, and years.
Communication is the exchange of information between people or systems.
A language is a structured system used to communicate meaning.
Observation is the act of collecting information through examination or measurement.
Evidence is information used to support or evaluate a claim.
A hypothesis is a proposed explanation that can be tested.
A model is a simplified representation of a system or process.
A measurement assigns a value to a property using a defined method and unit.
An experiment is a controlled investigation designed to test an idea.
A conclusion summarizes what can reasonably be inferred from evidence.
Critical thinking involves examining evidence, assumptions, alternatives, and reasoning.
A source is a place from which information is obtained.
Reliable information should be evaluated for accuracy, relevance, context, and evidence.
A fact is a statement that can be supported by appropriate evidence.
An opinion expresses a person's judgment, preference, or interpretation.
A question identifies information that needs to be investigated or explained.
Learning is the process of gaining knowledge or skills through study, experience, or instruction.
A skill is an ability developed through learning and practice.
A system is a collection of connected parts that interact to perform a function.
A process is a sequence of actions or transformations that produces an outcome.
A resource is something available for a purpose, such as time, energy, information, or computing capacity.
