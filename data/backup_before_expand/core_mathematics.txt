Addition combines quantities.
Subtraction finds a difference between quantities.
Multiplication can represent repeated addition or scaling.
Division distributes a quantity into equal parts or determines a quotient.
A fraction represents a ratio of integers or a part of a whole.
A percentage represents a value per one hundred.
A ratio compares quantities using division.
An equation states that two expressions have equal values.
A variable represents a value that may be unknown or change.
A constant has a fixed value within a defined problem.
An expression combines values, variables, and operations.
An exponent represents repeated multiplication of a base by itself for positive integer powers.
A square is a number multiplied by itself.
A square root is a number that produces a given value when squared.
A prime number has exactly two positive divisors: one and itself.
A composite number has more than two positive divisors.
Zero is an integer and is neither positive nor negative.
An even integer is divisible by two.
An odd integer is not divisible by two.
The perimeter of a rectangle is twice the sum of its length and width.
The area of a rectangle is length multiplied by width.
The area of a triangle is one half multiplied by base and height.
The circumference of a circle is related to its radius and the constant pi.
The area of a circle is pi multiplied by the square of its radius.
An angle measures rotation between two rays.
A triangle has three sides and three interior angles.
A right angle measures ninety degrees.
A linear equation can contain a variable to the first power.
An inequality compares values using symbols such as less than or greater than.
A function maps inputs to outputs according to a defined rule.
A coordinate system represents positions using numerical coordinates.
The Cartesian plane uses perpendicular horizontal and vertical axes.
A mean is calculated by dividing the sum of values by the number of values.
A median is the middle value after ordered data is considered, with averaging used for an even count.
A mode is a value that occurs most frequently in a dataset.
Probability measures how likely an event is under a defined probability model.
